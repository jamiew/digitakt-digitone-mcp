# Pop lens

Pop clarity comes from section function and hook hierarchy, not one fixed form.

- Make verse, prechorus, chorus, bridge, intro, and outro distinguishable by melody, register, density, harmony, or instrumentation.
- Keep a simple harmonic loop when melodic rhythm and register provide the interest.
- Strengthen the central title or hook in the chorus. Remove competing hooks before adding doubles.
- Use prosody: lyric stress, melodic stress, groove, harmony, and texture should point in the same emotional direction.
- Let the verse ask and the chorus answer. A prechorus should create direction, not merely add layers.
- Change at least two surface features at a section boundary.

Test the song with melody plus groove, then with the full production. If the form disappears without effects, the hook or contrast needs work.
