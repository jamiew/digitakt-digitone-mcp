# Techno and house lens

Arrange an energy curve around a stable pulse. Phrase-scale changes matter more than constant new notes.

- Establish the kick and primary groove before adding tension.
- Move one dimension at a time: density, brightness, register, space, subdivision, or harmonic pressure.
- Build by shortening event spacing, opening filters, raising register, or increasing send level.
- Break down by removing anchors, not only lowering volume.
- Make the drop restore the foundation and introduce one clear new fact.
- Schedule changes in 4-, 8-, 16-, or 32-bar groups when that supports dance-floor prediction; break the grid deliberately, not accidentally.
- Give every automation lane a known start and reset. Avoid state that leaks into the next pattern.

A hybrid hip-hop/dance form can keep an eight-bar rap phrase inside larger 16- or 32-bar energy sections.
