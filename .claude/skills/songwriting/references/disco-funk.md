# Disco and funk lens

The rhythm section is composition. Establish kick and bass as a reliable foundation, then interlock short guitar, keys, percussion, and vocal responses around it.

- Favor short accented figures over continuous pads.
- Keep the bass and kick relationship intentional. Lock important attacks; let pickups create propulsion.
- Use velocity, articulation, and small timing differences to preserve human motion.
- Leave holes. A few important parts can sound larger than many constant layers.
- Use the breakdown to reveal how the groove is built. Remove layers, expose the foundation or percussion, then rebuild in a clear order.
- Use open hats and cymbal state to signal energy, but keep choke behavior and levels consistent.

Nile Rodgers' production lens: a strong bottom supports artistic freedom; few instruments can sound important; technology should support human feel.
