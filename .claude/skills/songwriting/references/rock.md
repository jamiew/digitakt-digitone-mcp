# Rock lens

Protect the riff or vocal identity. Treat the band as changing combinations of rhythm section, harmonic support, riff, lead, and texture.

- Verse contrast can come from articulation and dynamics, not new chords.
- Let guitar, keys, and vocal occupy different rhythmic and register roles.
- Use cymbal state, guitar width, vocal doubles, and bass activity to raise or lower section energy.
- Make drum fills announce boundaries, then let the next downbeat deliver the payoff.
- Test the song on a reduced arrangement. If the form or hook disappears, production is masking a writing problem.
- Use bridge material to change perspective, register, harmony, or rhythmic feel before the final return.

Riff-driven and verse-chorus forms are both valid. Choose the form that makes the identity return feel inevitable.
